Let BB browse, click, fill out forms, and check web pages for you. BB works in a remote browser that runs on the machine where your thread lives, or on another machine connected to BB.

Watch BB work in live inline previews in the chat, and expand a preview for a closer look. Open the remote browser in the side panel to click, type, scroll, and switch tabs yourself while BB works. The remote browser keeps its logins, cookies, and tabs on each machine between sessions, so you and BB share one signed-in browser.

Enable Browser Automation in plugin settings, then ask BB to open a page, test a flow, or verify a change.
