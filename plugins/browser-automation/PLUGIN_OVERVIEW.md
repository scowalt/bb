Let BB browse, click, fill out forms, and check web pages for you. Control the in-app browser or run headless browsers on your local and remote machines connected to BB.

Headless sessions include live inline previews in the chat, so you can watch BB work and expand the preview for a closer look. Open the remote browser in the side panel to click, type, scroll, and switch tabs yourself while BB works. Headless browsers keep their logins, cookies, and tabs on each machine between sessions, so you and BB share one signed-in browser. In-app browser sessions stay visible in BB's browser panel.

Enable Browser Automation in plugin settings, then ask BB to open a page, test a flow, or verify a change.
